Sweet Rules 🧁

Before you grab your scoop, please read our sweet little guidelines to keep the store cozy for everyone.

🍦 Sweet Scoops: Our General Guidelines

  • We open our doors to all Gen!RP and Selective. Bring your unique flavor and join the fun!
  • Stick to one head per account. Make sure it's your main account. Side accounts are welcome, provided they stay as active as the main one.
  • Username, bio, and display name must be relevant to your muse.
  • Love wins!
  • You can update your character manually or by retweeting official posts, but manual updates show your love for the muse!
  • Limit your OAs to a maximum of 3, including us, and SQs to 2. Group DM's squat don't count.
  • Keep your social circle sweet—cap your following at 600. New accounts must show activity with 250 tweets and at least 50 followings.
  • Absolutely no JFB, nickname/upfoll schemes, bots, or drama-seeking. Keep it chill! Please keep RL talk inside brackets and avoid interacting with non-RP content.
  • NSFW content of any kind is strictly not allowed in our store. Let's keep this a cozy and comfortable space for everyone!
  • Don't be a wallflower! Interact with at least 5 Scoomies daily. We encourage a wide circle, so avoid CIC habits.
  • Active participation in games and missions is required. If you’re busy, just give us a heads-up!
  • Must follow our main base, sub-base, and your fellow OAs once you're verified.
  • Respect is the secret ingredient. Let’s keep this environment kind and delicious for everyone.

❄️ Chilling Out: Activity & Status Rules

  • Semi Hiatus max 3 days, and Hiatus max 5 days. Longer breaks aren’t possible here.
  • Temporary character swap is done a maximum 1x during the store, excluding other OA missions.
  • Account move is only allowed maximum 2 times. Exception applies only if your account gets suspended or locked.
  • Keep us in the loop if you change username, character, or move account.
  • Username or move account is allowed 1 week after verification.
  • Character change is allowed 3 days after verification.

⚠️ Don’t Let the Scoop Melt!: Warning & Unverification

  • Drama, CIC, war, or breaking rules leads to instant unverification.
  • Inactive for 24 hours after verification or hiatus period ends: unverified.
  • Inactive for 3 days or 72 hours without notice: unverified.
  • Deactivating your account without permission requires notifying us within 24 hours to avoid unverification.
  • Not participating in activities twice without notice will result in a warning.
  • Three warnings will lead to unverification.
Keep your scoop from melting: stay active, stay kind, and keep the store sweet. 🍨
